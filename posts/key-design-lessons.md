## Origins

In this short video, I explain three key ways interior designer Lucy Cunningham has made the Sitting Room in her Gloucestershire Manor House project so fantastically appealing.

You will learn how to highlight your home's architectural features by using subtle colors and patterns while incorporating statement pieces to create a balanced room.

You will also discover four simple ways—inspired by Lucy's relaxed country-house style—to make your home feel more comfortable and inviting.

Finally, we will take a quick look at how Lucy used lighting to maximize practicality and create atmosphere.
