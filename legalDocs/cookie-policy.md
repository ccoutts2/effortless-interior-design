# Cookie Policy

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. At _Effortless Interior Design_, we, along with some third-party service providers, use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. These cookies may include persistent cookies (which remain on your hard drive for an extended period of time) or session cookies (which expire when you close your browser).

In addition to cookies, we also use other tracking technologies, such as web beacons (also called “tracking beacons” or “clear gifs”) and local storage. These tiny graphic files contain a unique identifier that helps us recognize when someone visits our website or interacts with emails we send.

For more general information on cookies, you can visit [All About Cookies](https://www.allaboutcookies.org/) and [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/).

## How we use cookies

Cookies help us operate our website and provide services to you. Specifically, they can:

- Make your online experience more efficient and enjoyable, including recognizing you when you return to our website and adjusting the website based on your interests.
- Improve and customize your browsing experience, such as speeding up searches.
- Enable us to conduct research and perform analytics, helping us understand how users interact with our website and improving its functionality.

Third-party cookies may also be used to provide certain features on or through our website, such as analytics. These third parties are responsible for the cookies they set on our website, and we do not have control over them.

## What cookies do we use?

We may use the following types of cookies:

- **Operationally necessary cookies**: These cookies are essential for the operation of our website, including maintaining security and ensuring that key functions work properly.
- **Performance cookies**: These cookies help us understand how users navigate through our website. They track usage, number of visits per page, and user behavior, allowing us to optimize your browsing experience.

We also use Google Analytics to monitor how our website is used. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and generates reports detailing metrics such as the number of visitors to the site, general location data, time spent on the website, and the specific pages visited. These cookies do not collect personal data. If you prefer, you can disable persistent cookies in your browser to prevent Google Analytics from tracking your visits.

In addition, our website may feature social media buttons and plugins that allow you to connect with your social network in various ways. For these to function, the relevant social media platforms may set cookies on your device through our website. These cookies may contribute to the data held by those platforms in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## How can you control cookies?

You can manage the use of cookies by adjusting your web browser's settings. Please note that if you choose to reject cookies, your experience on our website may be limited, and some features may not work as intended.

To change your cookie settings or to receive notifications before cookies are used, adjust the settings in your web browser to prevent cookies from being stored on your device. You can also opt out of targeted advertising through most advertising networks. For more information, visit:

- [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/)
- [About Ads](http://www.aboutads.info/choices/)

For detailed instructions on managing cookies in different browsers, refer to the following resources:
